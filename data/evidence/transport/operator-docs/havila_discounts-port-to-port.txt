Port-to-port discounts

Some voyagers are eligible for port-to-port discounts. Here are all the details.

Information

All discounts apply to port-to-port journeys and are based on the standard distance fare, excluding cabin, car, and meals.

Discounts are not granted retrospectively and cannot be combined. Discounts do not apply to Round Voyages or other package and promotional offers.

General discount conditions
Applies to port-to-port travels
Applies to the distance fare
Applies to FLEX-ticket
Applies year round

Please note that some journeys booked between 0 and 30 days before departure may be cheaper than the discounted (FLEX) fare.

Details
Children 50%

The discount applies to:

Children aged 4 through 15.

Children under 4 who do not use their own berth travel free of charge, but must be included in the booking. If they use their own berth, the fare is calculated as for children over 4.

Senior citizens and disability pension recipients 50%

The discount applies to:

Persons over 67 years of age
Blind persons
Recipients of disability benefits
Disabled refugees receiving supplementary benefits
Persons receiving a disability pension under the National Insurance Act with a disability level of 50% or more, as well as persons receiving equivalent disability benefits from another EEA country

To receive the discount, a senior citizen card or other valid documentation must be presented.

Companion 100%

A companion receives a discount on the distance-travel ticket when travelling with a person who can present a companion card issued by their municipality of residence. The above discount schemes cannot be combined; however, the companion discount still applies even if the person holding the companion certificate is entitled to a discount themselves.

Military personnel / civilian service conscripts (Norwegian Armed Forces only) 50%

The military discount applies to conscripted military personnel serving in the Norwegian Armed Forces, specifically:

Soldiers completing initial military service
Travel to report for, and return home upon discharge from, initial military service
Travel to and from military selection
Military apprentices in their first and second year of education
Travel to report for/return home from new assignment in the Home Guard, or to attend/return from refresher service
Pupils and students at the Norwegian Defence University College

A call-up letter (valid only for travel to report for initial military service, new assignment, and Home Guard refresher exercises), student ID for the Norwegian Military Academy, or the Norwegian Armed Forces’ blue ID card are the only accepted proof of eligibility. The military discount applies only to Norwegian citizens affiliated with the Norwegian Armed Forces.

Students, apprentices and school pupils 50%

The discount applies to:

Students, school pupils, international students, and apprentices up to 30 years of age

A photo school ID must be presented. The course of study must have a continuous duration of at least three months; correspondence schools, short evening courses, specialist courses, and government agency schools do not qualify.
