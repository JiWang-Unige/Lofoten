Port-to-port travel

A port-to-port trip lets you choose your own route and travel between selected ports, whether you are visiting a particular destination or simply exploring a section of the coast. It is a flexible way to travel, but requires a little more planning than our packaged voyages.

Your ticket covers the selected port-to-port journey. Cabins, meals, vehicles and other extras can be added separately, where available. For a more straightforward trip with accommodation and experiences included, our packaged voyages may be a better fit.

Important information

Food is not included for port-to-port trips. If you want breakfast, lunch, and dinner, you can add meal packages when booking online (15% discount) or buy it from the café on board. Check-in opens when the ship docks. Generally, the latest check-in time is no later than 15 minutes before departure. In ports with shorter stops, check-in starts when the doors open.

You must book your trip at least 2 hours before the ship arrives at port
You can choose between a deckchair or a cabin when travelling overnight
If you want breakfast, dinner and lunch, you must book these as extras
You cannot book port-to-port trips earlier than 6 months before departure
