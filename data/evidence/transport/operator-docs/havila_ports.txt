Ports

The voyage between Bergen and Kirkenes visits 34 ports during the full 12-day stretch.

All ports
Information

Our four ships sail north and south between Kirkenes and Bergen all year round. The voyage takes in 34 ports. We dock in some ports during the day, and you can disembark for a few hours to experience the city. In other ports we only have a short stop with time to stretch your legs on the dock, and we sail past a number of ports at night while you are sleeping. On the journey north and south we stop at different ports, so you should choose a voyage that stops in the ports that you want to experience.
